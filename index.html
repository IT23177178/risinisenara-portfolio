Hi, I'm Risini Senara — a UI/UX designer. I design digital experiences that are simple, clear, and thoughtful to use. I care about how things feel just as much as how they look, and I approach design with attention to detail and intention.

My process usually begins with exploration and rough wireframes, followed by building high-fidelity designs in tools like Figma. I often step away and return with a fresh perspective to identify gaps, refine the experience, and iterate based on feedback.

I'm constantly learning, observing, and improving my approach to create work that feels natural and considered.
